About the cafe

A comfortable place to take a break

About the Cafe A patisserie and a pizzeria, deliberately under one roof. Sugar Rue began as a small dessert kitchen obsessed with laminated dough and seasonal fruit. Peppes Cafe grew up around a stone oven and a proper espresso machine. When the two met, we decided not to choose — so we built a room where you can start with a croissant, stay for a pizza, and finish with cake.
